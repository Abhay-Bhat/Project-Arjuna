❄️ Skadi

Forged in isolation. Driven by discipline.

Skadi is a comprehensive dashboard designed to help you structure your days, track your progress across six life domains, and conquer your UPSC preparation while building a fulfilling life.

📚 UPSC — Civil Service exam prep (586 classes)
💰 Finance — Dubai savings goal (199,800 AED)
❤️ Health — Sleep, gym, cholesterol tracking
🧠 Mind — Pastime streaks, loneliness tracking
🌱 Growth — Career milestones, books, reviews
⏱️ Routine — Daily schedule & habit tracking

How to Use Skadi

📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows your progress across all 6 domains at a glance. You can click any domain card to jump to that tab. The dashboard can be toggled (collapsed) to focus on your daily routine.

📋 Daily Routine

Each day has a tailored schedule based on your current life phase. Check off activities as you complete them. Your progress is tracked and displayed in real-time.

⏰ Phase-Based Schedules

Your plan spans 8 phases from May 2026 to May 2028. Each phase has its own schedule, tips, and milestones. The system automatically adjusts based on your current phase.

Key Concepts

🎯 Phases & Milestones

Notice Period (May–Jun 2026) → Dubai Settle (Jul–Sep) → Foundation (Oct–Mar) → Prelims Sprint (Apr–May 2027) and more. Each phase has specific goals.

🔥 Pastime Streak

Track your mental discipline with a daily streak counter. The Mind tab logs resets with reasons for pattern analysis.

💎 Data Persistence

All your data is saved in your browser's local storage. Your progress persists across sessions. You can export your data anytime from the settings.

Quick Start

You're all set! Here's how to get the most from Skadi:

✅ Start here:

  • 📝 Log today's sleep (Health tab or quick check-in)
  • ✓ Mark completed activities in your routine
  • 📚 Pick an UPSC subject and start a class
  • 🧠 Check the Mind tab to start a Pastime streak

💡 Pro tip: Use the tabs to navigate between domains. Dark/Light theme toggle is in the header. Questions? Look for help icons (?) on each tab.
